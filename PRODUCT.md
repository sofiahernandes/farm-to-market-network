# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Consumers buying locally produced food, who need to browse offers, manage a basket, check out, and track orders.

## Product Purpose

AgriTech connects customers with food from small local producers, from catalogue discovery through delivery tracking.

## Positioning

The product makes availability, origin, unit, pricing, delivery windows, and sandbox order state visible in one continuous purchasing journey.

## Operating Context

The experience includes public catalogue and product routes, account access, a consumer area, basket, checkout, orders, and order detail. Its backend and payment gateway are explicitly sandboxed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product catalogue data, basket state, checkout, and orders come from the API.
- Authentication and account creation are available.
- Preserve all current behavior and product copy exactly during frontend design work.
- The existing green, cream, and muted natural palette is a binding visual reference.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named AgriTech. Its voice is direct, clear, local, and transparent; it should not obscure the sandbox status of payments or backend data.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing Portuguese product copy and route implementations in `frontend/app/`; no photographic brand asset library is present in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Make origin and availability understandable at a glance.
- Keep the purchase path calm and legible.
- Let delivery and order state feel concrete.
- Be transparent about sandbox behavior.
